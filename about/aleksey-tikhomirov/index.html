Aleksey Tikhomirov

Founder & CEO, TerraLogicX

Aleksey Tikhomirov is the founder and CEO of TerraLogicX, an intelligence platform focused on the physical infrastructure of AI data centers.

Areas of focus

His work examines the relationship between land, power availability, permitting, and the timeline to commercial operation. Key areas include data center site selection, land due diligence, grid interconnection, and Time-to-Power risk.

TerraLogicX

TerraLogicX analyzes infrastructure constraints that affect site readiness and project economics. Its research considers land rights, easements, zoning, substation capacity, water availability, and evidence supporting power delivery schedules.
