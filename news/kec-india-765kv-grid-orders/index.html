KEC Wins INR 28.72 Billion in Indian Grid Orders

765 kV transmission projects linked to 8.5 GW of renewable generation highlight the infrastructure between generation and usable power.

Adapted from the Telegram editorial archive dated 2026-10-25. English edition published 2026-10-26.

What was announced

KEC International announced on 24 October that it had secured INR 2,872 crore — INR 28.72 billion — in turnkey orders from Power Grid Corporation of India. The scope covers design, supply, and installation of 765 kV transmission lines. According to KEC, the projects are associated with the evacuation of 8.5 GW of renewable energy from Gujarat.

Why it matters for infrastructure

Generating capacity is not the same as electricity delivered where it is needed. Transmission provides the connection between new generation and wider demand. For data center site selection, this distinction matters: regional generation additions do not automatically establish available capacity at a particular substation.

What the announcement does not establish

These are contract awards, not completed lines or confirmed data center connections. The release does not establish the commissioning date, delivered output, project margin, or power availability for any individual compute campus.

Takeaway

Track network construction alongside generation. Before attaching a power premium to land, verify the relevant connection, delivery milestones, and contractual conditions.
